Privacy Policy

1. Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy describes how Huzaifa Malik ("exhuzaifa", "we", "us", operating under Techsynx) manages personal data collected through the portfolio website hosted at https://exhuzaifa.vercel.app/. We take privacy seriously and adhere to transparent, minimal-data principles.

2. What Information We Collect

We only collect information that you deliberately provide to us:

  • Direct Communications: If you reach out via email (exhuzaifa@gmail.com), phone, or WhatsApp, we retain the communication for business correspondence.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process your contact details exclusively to:

  • Engage in professional communications and contract fulfillment.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to any third parties for advertising or commercial surveillance.

4. Cookies & Web Tracking

This site operates on an essential-only cookie policy. We do not load third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts. Essential local caching may be utilized strictly to maintain application state and smooth scroll animations.

5. Subprocessors & Infrastructure Providers

To securely host and deliver this portfolio, we utilize reputable, enterprise-grade infrastructure providers:

  • Vercel Inc.: Hosting, Edge CDN, and secure content delivery.
  • Web3Forms: Encrypted transmission of contact form messages directly to our inbox.
  • GitHub (Microsoft): Open-source code repository hosting.

6. Data Retention & User Rights

Inquiry data is retained only for the duration necessary to address your project request and maintain ongoing professional relationships. You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of any personal data you have shared with us.

To exercise your rights, email us directly at exhuzaifa@gmail.com. We will acknowledge and process your request within 7 business days.

7. Security Safeguards

All communication with this website is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). For security vulnerability inquiries and disclosures, please refer to our Security Policy.
